About Us

More Than an Alumni Network. A Lifelong Community.

The Mahatma Alumni Association brings together alumni across every campus and batch, creating opportunities to reconnect, contribute, and celebrate the journey that shaped us. Our community spans generations, professions, and geographies, united by the values that define Mahatma.
