## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the living legacy, dignity, and intergenerational fellowship of a storied Indian educational institution. The aesthetic rejects transient digital tropes—ephemeral gradients, floating glassomorphic sheets, and corporate SaaS sterility—in favor of an editorial, archive-grade experience grounded in history, warmth, and enduring honor.

The visual direction marries classical institutional prestige with the intimate narrative of lifelong alumni kinship. It feels tactile, thoughtful, and historic: reminiscent of handcrafted convocation folios, aged architectural brick and limestone, archival yearbooks, and distinguished varsity libraries. The emotional resonance is proud yet deeply welcoming, celebrating both monumental achievements and personal memories across decades of graduating cohorts.

## Colors

The color palette reflects tradition, dignified authority, and warm fellowship:

- **Midnight (`#1F2A44`)**: The anchor of authority. Utilized for deep structural headers, monumental footers, authoritative typography, and commanding editorial callout blocks.
- **Heritage Gold (`#C6A75E`)**: A restrained, metallic accent evoking architectural brass plaques, honors crests, fine 1px dividers, bespoke line-art iconography, and refined badges.
- **Ivory (`#E8DCC8`)**: The warm tactile surface reminiscent of aged vellum and handmade archival parchment, utilized across broad canvas sections to disrupt digital coldness.
- **Legacy Blue (`#2D637F`)**: Secondary editorial depth, applied to chapter spotlights, dynamic portal panels, and secondary interactive states.
- **Honor Gold (`#E09E19`)**: An energetic ceremonial accent reserved for Homecoming focal points, urgent giving campaigns, and active primary event CTAs.
- **Supporting Foundations**:
  - **Graphite (`#333333`)**: Deep neutral body text, calibrated for effortless readability against light surfaces without the severe harshness of pure black.
  - **Slate Grey (`#53626F`)**: Muted metadata, captions, and understated secondary copy.
  - **Stone Mist (`#C2B9A7`)**: Structural borders, understated hairline rules, and inactive controls.
  - **Soft Grey (`#F3F3F3`)**: Neutral alternating container backgrounds.
  - **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Crisp, elevated card planes and photographic matting.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances literary dignity with sharp, architectural precision:

- **Primary Editorial Serifs (`Noto Serif`)**: Anchors all major headlines, historical chapter markers, commemorative dates, and institutional proclamations. Regular and semi-bold weights preserve literary elegance without appearing frail.
- **Architectural Geometric Sans (`DM Sans`)**: Chosen for its balanced geometric proportions and high legibility across navigation, body text, data matrices, forms, and functional buttons.
- **Sub-Headings & Overlines**: Uppercase letter-spaced sans labels (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) set in Heritage Gold or Midnight provide structure to archival classifications, cohort numbers, and geographic chapter divisions.
- **Storytelling Accents**: Restrained, editorial callout styling uses italicized serif weights to reflect intimate personal stories, donor inscriptions, and memories recorded across the years.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors classical broadsheet publishing and museum editorial layouts:

- **Asymmetric Grid System**: Built on a 12-column grid desktop framework that deliberately embraces asymmetry. Column spans alternate between substantial narrative panels (7 columns) paired alongside archival photographic plates or metadata summaries (5 columns).
- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Generous section margins (`margin: 4rem` on desktop, adapting down to `1.25rem` on mobile) allow complex heritage photography and narrative prose to breathe.
- **Section Breaks**: Generous vertical spacing distinguishes eras, annual reunions, and leadership boards. Sections shift alternately from crisp white surfaces to deep Midnight fields and grounded Ivory backdrops.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering, border work, and tangible matting rather than artificial multi-layer drop shadows:

- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Divisions are predominantly rendered using precise 1px borders in Heritage Gold (`#C6A75E`), Stone Mist (`#C2B9A7`), or transparent Midnight (`rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.08)`).
- **Physical Matting**: Photographic elements rely on framed borders (such as 4px or 8px solid Ivory/White framing with a hairline outline) to echo physical mountings in archival exhibition galleries.
- **Surface Elevation**: Layering relies on planar contrast—Ivory panels sitting flush over Midnight fields, or pure White cards resting upon Soft Grey or Ivory planes.
- **Subtle Ambient Shadows**: Interactive components and modal dialogs use minimal, diffused, warm-tinted shadows: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.08)`. Never use cold, synthetic blue or purple drop shadows.

## Shapes

Shapes are architectural, deliberate, and dignified:

- **Corner Radii**: A strict radius of `8px` to `12px` (`roundedness: 2`) defines the core tactile language. Large pill-shaped buttons and extreme rounds are explicitly disallowed, preserving an architectural sense of permanence.
- **Badges & Chapter Emblems**: Small metadata badges and event chips utilize disciplined `6px` or `8px` corners.
- **Icon Enclosures**: Line icons are framed within refined circular medallions (`rounded-full`) outlined with 1px Heritage Gold hairlines and subtle Ivory or Midnight tints.
- **Image Framing**: Documentary and historical photography employs crisp corners with optional high-contrast inner borders or generous passe-partout matting.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Institutional Button**: Solid Midnight (`#1F2A44`) fill with pure White text, 8px to 10px corner radius, and subtle 1px Heritage Gold internal or boundary highlights. Hover transitions deepen to a richer dark tone with a slight translation.
- **Heritage / Ceremonial Button**: Solid Honor Gold (`#E09E19`) or Heritage Gold (`#C6A75E`) fill with Midnight text, reserved for critical campaigns (e.g., Annual Homecoming, Scholarship Giving).
- **Secondary / Outlined Button**: Transparent fill with a 1.5px Heritage Gold or Midnight border and matching high-contrast label.

### Cards & Narrative Tiles
- **Alumni Story & Profile Card**: Structured on a crisp White or warm Ivory base with a 1px Stone Mist border. The image container sits flush at the top or within an offset matted frame. Titles are set in `headline-sm` serif, accompanied by small caps cohort badges (e.g., "CLASS OF '84").
- **Archival Milestone Card**: High-contrast, Midnight background card accented with 1px Heritage Gold framing, utilized for milestone memorials, endowments, and historic timelines.

### Chips & Filters
- **Cohort & Chapter Chips**: Outlined rectangular pills with a 6px corner radius. Inactive states feature Slate Grey text with a Stone Mist border; active states feature solid Midnight fill with crisp White text or Heritage Gold fill with Midnight text.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp rectangular inputs with 8px radius, White background, and a 1px Stone Mist outline. On focus, the outline shifts decisively to Midnight accompanied by a subtle Heritage Gold glow (`0 0 0 1px #C6A75E`). Placeholders are set in muted Slate Grey.

### Dividers & Section Breaks
- **Ornamental Hairlines**: 1px horizontal rules tinted with Heritage Gold or Stone Mist. Important section dividers often feature an understated diamond or circular crest emblem centered within the rule.

### Archival Lists & Rosters
- **Directory Lists**: Alternating soft Ivory and White rows, separated by hairline borders. Names appear in semi-bold sans or medium serif typography, with dates and house affiliations aligned neatly in right-hand tabular columns.
