About Us

More Than an Alumni Network. A Lifelong Community.

The Mahatma Alumni Association brings together former students across every campus and batch — to stay connected, mentor the next generation and give back to the institution that shaped us. This community helps Mahatmites reconnect with one another, build meaningful relationships and continue a shared journey beyond school.
